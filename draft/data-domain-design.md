# Data Domain Design

!!! "Domain?"

    Business Capability that answer the same business question.

The data domain will split after Bronze zone.

## Domain Checker

The criteria that use to answer 3 questions:

| Domain     | Question 1                                   | Question 2                                 | Question 3                                                 |
|------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Inventory  | What is the inventory level of each product? | How many products are in stock?            | What is the total value of inventory?                      |
| Sales      | What are the total sales for each product?   | How many units of each product were sold?  | What is the total revenue generated from sales?            |
| Customer   | Who are our customers?                       | What are their demographics?               | What is their purchasing behavior?                         |
| Finance    | What are our total expenses?                 | What is our net profit?                    | What is our cash flow?                                     |
| Product    | What products do we offer?                   | What are the features of each product?     | What is the lifecycle of each product?                     |
| Promotion  | What promotions are currently active?        | How effective are our promotions?          | What is the ROI of our promotions?                         |
| HR         | Who are our employees?                       | What are their roles and responsibilities? | What is the employee turnover rate?                        |
| Assortment | What products are in our assortment?         | How are they categorized?                  | What is the performance of each product in the assortment? |
| Store      | Where are our stores located?                | What are their operating hours?            | What is the foot traffic in each store?                    |
| Supplier   | Who are our suppliers?                       | What products do they supply?              | What is the lead time for each supplier?                   |
| Logistics  | How are our products transported?            | What are the delivery times?               | What is the cost of logistics?                             |
| Operations | What are our operational processes?          | How efficient are they?                    | What is the cost of operations?                            |
| Marketing  | What marketing channels do we use?           | How effective are our marketing campaigns? | What is the ROI of our marketing efforts?                  |
